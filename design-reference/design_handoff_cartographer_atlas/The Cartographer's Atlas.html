From the desk of Claude Design

This is the first plate of Liber II — not a mock, but a real continental world the engine drew at seed 73, rendered so you can judge how it should read. The land is computed first; the magic is a single field laid over it — the Winds, with no affinity — held back to a quiet layer, exactly as Opus set down. My job here is the one decision §VII left open — how the terrain is made legible — plus the glyph hand, the palette, and the manner of the magic.

  • LeanGlyphs as the type channel — unambiguous landform & cover — laid optionally over hex-quantised relief so the land has form you can feel.
  • HandA confident engraver's line: bold, rounded, open. Reads as texture at continental scale, as iconography up close.
  • GroundDuck-egg sea, parchment land — the aged-atlas palette, true to Liber I.
  • MagicThe Winds blow everywhere, unevenly — a few strong wells by default; the mana field is yours to summon as a wash, never the base coat.
  • Built to changeChannel and magic are live dials (open Tweaks). Every colour is a hook, not a baked fill — re-theme without re-running the engine.

V · The Quiet Layer

The Winds of Magic

The land speaks first. There is no affinity — magic is one fuel, the Winds, that blows across the whole world and pools unevenly. A few strong wells stand by default; the mana field itself is yours to summon as a wash.
